# My core beliefs

These statements reflect my personal values and my commitment to human rights, international law, and ethical practice. They are not intended to prescribe policy positions, but to provide context for how I approach inclusive and responsible work.

I strongly believe in the following:

## Equality and human rights

- Gender equality
- LGBTQ+ rights
- Body autonomy
- Disability rights are human rights
- Racial equity
- Refugees are not criminals, they have rights

## Universal services and basic needs

- Universal healthcare
- Mental health care is healthcare
- Free education for all
- Everyone deserves access to clean water and food
- No one should be homeless — housing is a human right

## Economic justice and fair work

- Workers deserve fair wages and safe conditions
- Corporations and the wealthy should pay their fair share
- Capitalism puts profit over people and the planet

## Democracy, law, and justice systems

- Democracy must be protected and expanded
- Prisons should focus on rehabilitation, not punishment
- The right of people everywhere to self-determination, democratic governance, and protection under international law

## Anti-colonialism, peace, and international solidarity

- Colonialism and its ongoing impacts must be dismantled
- War and occupation are not solutions
- The right of Indigenous peoples to self-determination, autonomy over their institutions, and secure ownership and stewardship of their lands must be honoured in law and practice
- Palestine is entitled to international recognition as a sovereign state with the same rights to self-govern as any other nation
