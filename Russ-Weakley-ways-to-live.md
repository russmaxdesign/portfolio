# Ways to live

## Reduce harm + empathy

- Treat others as you would want to be treated
- In all things, try to minimise harm
- Avoid unnecessary suffering, including to animals
- Treat people with love, honesty, faithfulness and respect
- Respect people’s consent
- Do not commit violence against another human unless there is no other alternative

## Truth and intellectual honesty

- Be open-minded and update beliefs with evidence
- Seek what is likely true
- Question everything

## Responsibility

- Be truthful, and avoid deception unless it prevents greater harm
- Be mindful of the consequences of your actions
- Take responsibility for what you do
- Leave the world better than you found it
